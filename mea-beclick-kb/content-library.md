# Content library and weekly review

The VPS plans one daily organic post and prepares seven verified videos for each Sunday review. Review each exact Drive preview, caption and time in the Content tab. Check Listened for videos with audio. Check Approve to authorize the exact release on connected Instagram and Facebook channels.

Daily Calendar shows the rolling plan and seven days of Buffer coverage. Review Log tracks weekly readiness and review time. [Operating guide](../marketing/content-library/MACHINE.md)
