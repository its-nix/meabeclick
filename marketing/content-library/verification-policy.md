# Encoded-output verification policy v1

Both independent roles must inspect all supplied evidence. Verify math and teaching, Hebrew,
phone readability, timing, motion trajectory and landing, brand, hook and payoff, caption/CTA.
Never infer a creative pass from technical QA, implementation source, source rationale or approval.
Dense coverage is at least 5 fps plus first/final original frames. Motion evidence conservatively
covers the full clip at original frame rate, with original frame indices and timestamps retained.
At most 1800 frames / 60 seconds, constant 24–60 fps. No listening inference. Arbitrary audio blocks. Reproducible original procedural instrumental music may pass only with exact encoded waveform identity, regenerated provenance, full decode and measured -35..-18 LUFS / at most -1 dBTP. Human listening remains pending and required. Stills do not constitute continuous video playback.
Each pass requires every check explicitly pass, no findings/patches, complete evidence coverage,
current policy/media/context identity, and two distinct fresh reviewer sessions. Owner approval
is never implied. Unsupported motion/source restructuring escalates. Corrections use only the
existing safe text/CSS/numeric patch engine, isolated source copies, new immutable children,
and at most two persisted rounds. Missing evidence/auth, timeouts and interrupted calls block.

Only material defects block: incorrect or misleading teaching/motion, illegible content, broken continuity, unsupported claims, or missing purposeful opening/payoff/next step. Optional polish, unmeasured retention predictions and taste alone are not P1 defects.

Dense 5fps evidence must use native-resolution original-frame pairs (left then right), with hashes and exact original indices. Retain the all-original-frame motion sheets. Reviewers must trace moving objects against visible paths/guides, shadows and landing targets throughout travel, not only compare endpoints. No supplied defect timestamps or implementation hints are permitted. More than100 attachments including endpoint originals blocks; no evidence thinning.

Enumeration teaching must maintain correspondence between each visible counter/label and its associated highlighted object/category throughout intermediate states. Material count/highlight mismatch or gaps implying a missing step block, even when the final total is correct. Legitimate transitions are not defects by themselves.
