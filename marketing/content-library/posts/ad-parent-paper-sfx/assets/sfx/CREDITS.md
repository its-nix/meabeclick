# Sound sources

Only these two recordings are used. Both are marked CC0; the links and hashes are retained for provenance.

- `page-turn.mp3`: “Page Turn (2)” by OwlStorm / Ashe Kirk / Owlish Media. [Source](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/151221/), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The public high-quality preview is used; short excerpts are trimmed, filtered, faded and gain-adjusted.
- `wood-click.mp3`: “wood-click-3.wav” by husamalhomsi (the Openverse catalog retains the name Sawuare). [Source](https://freesound.org/s/500927/), [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Retrieved through Openverse; short excerpts are filtered, faded and attenuated.

The earlier CC-BY page-turn search result was not selected and is not present in this project or its output. No bundled synth sound, generated music, narration or cloned voice is used.
