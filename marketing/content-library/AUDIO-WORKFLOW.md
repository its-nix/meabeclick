# Audio iteration workflow

## Production music exception —2026-11-15

The feedback worker can synthesize light original procedural instrumental music without a voice provider/GPU or external music purchase. It verifies reproducible PCM provenance, encoded AAC identity, loudness/peak limits, full decode and exact job/media binding. This is technical/source verification, not a claim that an agent listened to the music. Owner Listened remains required before approval. Arbitrary audio/narration still blocks without its separate evidence path. Current centering/music/caption preferences are persisted through the feedback ledger and generated creative rules; they are not temporary manual edits.


Narration is optional. A visually complete post can move from approved creative plus caption/CTA review to separately authorized release without a voice, local GPU or audio approval. Voice testing is a parallel branch, not a launch dependency. Music/effects are optional enhancements; the message must remain clear with sound off.

Keep the current local Mac Hebrew model/accepted synthetic voice as the baseline when using that branch. Change one component at a time. Preserve the approved silent educational candidates and the original ad comparison; each new audio/copy combination is a separate artifact with a parent.

## Optional audio stages and shared release gates

| Stage | What changes | Review gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silent creative | Hook, explanation, motion and next step | Owner visual feedback, math/Hebrew/mobile readability |
| Narration only | Script, timing and existing local voice performance | Wording diagnostic, levels/timing, then owner listening |
| Music | One selected bed; narration optional | Owner comparison with the same visuals and any accepted voice; verify rights and intelligibility |
| Effects | Sounds attached to visible actions | Compare against the accepted visual/audio control; remove distracting effects |
| Complete post | Silent or audio video, external subtitles when applicable, caption and CTA | Owner approves the exact publishable package; silent posts skip audio gates |
| Release and learning | Actual account/date/post or ad ID and observation window | Separate publication/spend authority; record actual results and review minutes |

The 30-minute weekly review remains one batch. Review only the changed part; carry forward earlier approvals without treating them as approval of new audio. Never infer campaign performance from owner taste. Current cadence, account settings, music choice, spend and publication timing are not invented by this workflow.
