# Visual math teaching: skills and rendering workflows

Research checked 2026-11-10 against first-party documentation, source repositories and this implementation. Research only: nothing installed, no product code or customer data changed. Recommendations are proposals for the owner interview.

## What constrains the current result

The current corpus has only6–8 scenes per lesson. Percentages and the official integral example use only `steps`; quadratics uses only `quadratic`. Only the integral example has a verified official-source reference. `learningProblems` caps an experience at12 scenes. V2 expands the visual enum to ten numeric visual families, but a frame remains caption/TeX/numeric values. It has no general slide composition, illustration asset, annotated source figure or geometric transformation description. Sources: `src/lib/learning/model.ts`, `schema.ts`, `server/lesson/prep.ts`, and `server/learning-review-v2-corpus.json`.

The production prompt invokes shared `teachingRules`; installing a local slide skill does not automatically change that output model or renderer. `LEARNING_INSTRUCTIONS` explicitly forbids generated HTML and design instructions and delegates appearance to the fixed template. The presentation guide even documents that the triangle visual cannot depict the three attached squares of a Pythagorean proof. Inference: stronger prompting can improve sequence and wording, but cannot deliver the requested compositional and mathematical visual variety without a richer rendering contract. Preserve the brand invariants while giving authoring meaningful, bounded layout and visual choices.

## Attractive tools with a commercial integration dependency

GeoGebra has real [graphing/geometry/3D embedding and state APIs](https://geogebra.github.io/docs/reference/en/GeoGebra_Apps_Embedding/), including self-hosting. Its [license](https://www.geogebra.org/license) explicitly treats revenue-oriented online education/platform use as commercial and requires a special agreement; personal classroom use is described separately. Mathigon/Amplify Polypad has excellent manipulatives, but [Mathigon's commercial-use FAQ](https://uk.mathigon.org/faqs) and [Polypad API terms](https://polypad.amplify.com/polypad-api-terms.pdf) describe commercial licensing/payment and integration restrictions. They are useful design references or future licensed options; neither should be embedded in this paid platform on the assumption that free teacher access grants platform rights.

## Recommended refinement to discuss

Keep the website brand and existing customer publication/progress system. Separate the teaching storyboard, slide composition, mathematical visual recipe and assessment evidence. Author many short teaching beats when useful; do not equate a new number on the navigator with a new insight. Make each interaction expose an invariant or contrast the learner can explain. Keep questions, working and solutions in controlled stages, with private notes grounded in the visible state.

Calibrate one representative full lesson against Nikol's actual rendered output before generating another ten. Evaluate the deck as a sequence and inspect the actual models while manipulating them. The new skill should require visual evidence and comparison, not use test count or valid JSON as a substitute for visual/teaching quality. Exact slide-count targets, illustration style, pace, audio and the balance of exploration versus exam practice remain owner decisions for the grilling round.

## Addendum: uploaded dependency manifest and agreed calibration

The expanded uploaded guide establishes its actual dependencies. `presentations:Presentations` supplies editable slide composition, template following, speaker notes, export and rendered-slide inspection. `pdf:pdf` (or an equivalent trusted workflow) is conditional on reading/cropping exam PDFs or producing a PDF. `imagegen` is optional for illustrative scenes; it must not generate official question images, logos, exact graphs or mathematical evidence. The manifest explicitly excludes `educational-design-system` as a runtime requirement. No additional animation engine or hidden mathematical teaching skill is declared.

Comparison with the earlier reference package found only two substantive changes: the new dependency manifest and an instruction to use the PDF workflow for inspecting/cropping official sources, rather than only PDF delivery. The four existing design, architecture, quality-control and mathematics references are unchanged apart from trailing whitespace. This confirms the intended authoring workflow; it does not establish that a new skill alone explains the reference decks' quality. The rendered artifacts still need direct comparison.

Transferable practices are concrete: preserve an approved reference's composition; use one teaching purpose per slide and split solutions; show a legible original question with its complete required diagram before editable working; independently solve every used subpart; render every slide and inspect the latest render. Mathematical source reading must never guess a sign, exponent or hidden label. Existing repository guides already contain most continuity, notes, notation and answer-checking rules. The missing contract chiefly concerns actual presentation composition, source-image assets and visual evidence. The uploaded palette, font defaults, fixed60-minute structure and three-unit assumptions remain subordinate to the website brand and learner profile.

The owner has agreed to roughly20–30 meaningful slides for90 minutes with the website brand preserved, original official questions/diagrams followed by annotations/reconstruction/solutions, and one complete calibration lesson before another ten. These require a slide-level composition model beyond the current12-scene limit; trusted readable source crops with accessible text and provenance; annotation layers separate from the original; and instructional/solution stages with appropriate learner/teacher disclosure. An uploaded deck's teacher-only notes convention is not permission to expose notes or keys through student HTML.

The next useful decision frontier is the calibration topic and exact audience/assessment target, plus the intended classroom presentation surface (a large slide canvas with quiet controls versus the present app-panel composition). If a visual style choice remains open after inspecting the uploaded decks, compare concrete alternatives. Source-question count and activity balance depend on the chosen lesson, so settle those afterward. Dynamic interaction should be judged by the mathematical insight it makes visible; do not substitute more navigator entries or decorative motion for that insight. No implementation or installation has been performed during this comparison.

## Rendered reference comparison

Three supplied examples were inspected as rendered decks:14 and22 slides from two16:9 PPTX files, plus an18-page16:9 PDF. PowerPoint sources were converted with LibreOffice for inspection; no claim of inspection in Microsoft PowerPoint is made. All54 slides were reviewed in overview, with representative diagram, source-question, worked-solution and recap slides examined at full size. Private source files and page renders remain outside tracked source.

The stronger continuation references give distinct roles to cover, timed map, retrieval, visual concept map, original exam-question reading, staged working, misconception checks and exit questions. Readable mathematical bands, two-column comparisons and source-image/working layouts provide useful variation. Their exact fonts, warm backgrounds and alternate logo are not the website's visual authority. Preserve the website logo, Heebo and palette while transferring the compositional hierarchy and pacing.

The references are useful benchmarks with limitations. A cumulative-percent label wraps awkwardly in one rendered slide, some full-question crops are dense, and the introductory deck has much less substantive speaker-note coverage than the continuation deck. Backgrounds and repeated logos account for many picture objects; raw image counts would be a misleading illustration-quality metric. Fresh materials must retain complete private notes and legible question/detail views. A visual must explain a mathematical relationship; branding and decoration do not count as a conceptual illustration.

Normal-distribution conventions vary between the references: one uses sigma and standard-score/table calculations; the other uses the school half-standard-deviation diagram and s notation. Select the appropriate teaching profile for the target course and paper. Do not merge their numerical approximation rules indiscriminately.

The normal questions were matched to the original Ministry of Education [summer2025 questionnaire35372, question3, page8](https://meyda.education.gov.il/sheeloney_bagrut/2025/6/HEB/35372.pdf) and [winter2025 questionnaire35372, question3, page8](https://meyda.education.gov.il/sheeloney_bagrut/2025/1/HEB/35372.pdf). Both PDFs were directly downloaded and visually inspected after web-reader failures. Used answers were recomputed independently using the stated school-diagram convention. Local evidence records document hashes, page identities and calculations; this inspection does not itself add a trusted production-catalogue record.

A proposed first calibration is a90-minute introduction and progression through school-profile normal distribution for the three-unit35372 target, using the longer reference's question treatment and the introductory reference's visual foundations. The pilot topic and learner starting point remain an owner decision; homework, game and source-question balance follow from that brief.
