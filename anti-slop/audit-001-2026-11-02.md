Method: dual-agent (A: /root/student_design_a · B: /root/student_evidence_b), plus primary-agent public-page and synthetic-family checks.

Date: 2026-11-02
Mode: antislop AFTER, selected explicitly by the user.
Implementation update: the user subsequently approved findings 1-15 and selected an operational tutor overview. Implemented changes and bounded verification are recorded in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-02-sitewide-ux-verification.md`; the audit below preserves the original pre-fix evidence.

Resolved scope: src/routes and linked interface components, not every production record.
No UI fixes, production writes, Todoist changes, new assets, or design-system replacement were authorized or performed.

## Verdict

The site is not consistently usable yet. Tutor tools have the largest functional and task-efficiency gaps. Parent information order favors access administration over learning understanding. Student navigation is substantially stronger, but the homework view repeats information.

The product has a distinctive mechanism: named tutors, individual lessons, skill plans, reports, generated materials and playable practice. Those capabilities are real product context, not evidence that every page expresses the user's next task well. Blue, cards, rounded corners and legitimate status indicators are not inherently defects.

## Priority findings

UX severity uses P0 blocking, P1 major, P2 meaningful but workable, P3 polish. Antislop tiers are separate: a LOW-tier composition rule can still have P1 task impact. No P0 was established. There are 8 P1 groups and 7 P2 groups. No P3 backlog is proposed.

### 1. P1: Tutor controls disappear on phones

Evidence: at 390px, the student payment table exceeded its card content area and its row actions were clipped approximately 21px by overflow:hidden. The five-tab strip had about 440px of content inside a 325px viewport; Profile was initially absent and Homework partially visible, without an overflow cue. Homework checkboxes measured 22x22px and were unnamed. Relevant form text computed to 14.08px. The mobile tab compression rule is overridden by the later base rule.

Locations: src/routes/app/dashboard/+page.svelte:1193, :1286, :1330, :1711, :1897, :1911, :1914, :1952.
Impact: the tutor cannot reliably discover or operate ordinary student actions on a phone.
Fix: use a phone-safe payment presentation with reachable actions, expose all navigation choices or a clear overflow affordance, use named native controls and larger hit areas, and meet PRODUCT's 16px input floor. Let learning-node labels wrap and relocate secondary information.
Antislop: R-03, R-32, R-39 (HIGH). Suggested workflow: impeccable adapt, then audit.
Standards distinction: the product goal is 44px targets. WCAG 2.2 AA minimum is 24px with exceptions; dimensions alone do not establish failure of its spacing exception.

### 2. P1: Library preparation work is buried below the catalog

Evidence: 212 catalog rows precede the learner-linked next-preparation disclosure, around 20,339px below desktop top and 28,328px on phone. At 390px, the table was 880px wide inside a 341px scroll region. Three filter controls began offscreen.
Locations: src/routes/app/library/+page.svelte:84, :109, :137, :174.
Impact: finding what to prepare next requires extensive scrolling and sideways exploration.
Fix: put upcoming learner needs first, keep filters outside the wide table, and bound or paginate the catalog.
Antislop: R-05, R-20 and C-3 (composition/LOW tier, major task impact). Suggested workflow: impeccable shape, layout.
This is contained table scrolling, not page-wide overflow or automatic WCAG failure.

### 3. P1: Lesson editing is an enormous form without a persistent save area

Evidence: one production master sample exposed 138 editable controls. Its first lesson save action was approximately 13,395px below desktop top and 13,513px on phone.
Locations: src/routes/app/lessons/[slug]/edit/+page.svelte:400, :577.
Impact: a tutor cannot comfortably edit a small part of a lesson and save without substantial traversal.
Fix: persistent draft/save/publish status and actions, with independently navigable or collapsible editing sections. Preserve the distinction between saving and publishing.
Antislop: R-05, R-20, C-3 (LOW tier, major task impact). Suggested workflow: impeccable shape, layout.
These measurements describe the sampled document, not every lesson.

### 4. P1: Unsaved work can be lost in two tutor workflows

Source evidence: onProfileFieldChange restores the old stored value after failed save, discarding the attempted note. Overview save success appears inside the separate Profile panel. editGame replaces the selected game draft and its saved baseline directly; the route-navigation guard does not protect this local game switch.
Locations: src/routes/app/dashboard/+page.svelte:652, :670, :1226, :1406; src/routes/app/lessons/[slug]/edit/+page.svelte:74, :207, :328.
Impact: freshly composed progress explanations or game changes can disappear.
Fix: retain attempted text as a recoverable draft, show saving/saved/error beside the field with retry, and preserve separate game drafts or ask before replacing a dirty one.
Antislop: R-27 and C-4 resilience; the game switch also concerns R-26 functional completeness. Suggested workflow: impeccable harden.
Failure writes and dirty-game replacement were not reproduced against production.

### 5. P1: Learning-plan filters and report-to-plan continuity are visually removed

Source evidence: five plan filters and PlanTree reside in legacy-plan-compat, which is display:none!important. The visible LearningPlanTree receives no filter. Report success navigates with filter=changed, but that filter drives the hidden view.
Locations: src/routes/app/plan/[code]/+page.svelte:424, :434, :543; src/routes/app/report/[booking]/+page.svelte:150.
Impact: the tutor cannot use the intended changed/review/recommended filtering workflow in the visible plan.
Fix: restore useful filters to the visible map and connect report completion to the skills that actually changed.
Antislop: R-26/R-27 continuity and R-20 hierarchy. Suggested workflow: impeccable harden, shape.
No visible filter was clicked and found failing; the finding is source-confirmed absence/wiring.

### 6. P1: Duplicate payment interfaces can present inconsistent state

Evidence: Payments exists both inside the student tabs and as a lower page section. Source update paths refresh different local stores. Live initial loading briefly showed settled status before replacing it with an outstanding-balance state.
Locations: src/routes/app/dashboard/+page.svelte:356, :820, :1247, :1519.
Impact: the tutor has to decide which representation to trust.
Fix: one ledger destination with shared confirmed state and explicit loading/error behavior. Keep manual payment recording, which PRODUCT deliberately chooses.
Antislop: R-27, C-2/C-4; composition R-05. Suggested workflow: impeccable shape, harden.
No payment mutation or numerical discrepancy was induced.

### 7. P1: Homepage drawer leaves hidden controls in keyboard navigation

Live reproduction: Shift+Tab from the menu opener focused a link inside the closed sidebar, beyond the visible viewport. Escape left the open drawer open. Source closes it only through transform and provides no complete focus/dismissal contract.
Locations: src/lib/components/Header.svelte:41, :71, :194.
Impact: keyboard users can lose their location in an invisible menu or move behind an open overlay.
Fix: make closed content unavailable to focus and accessibility APIs; implement appropriate disclosure/dialog semantics, Escape and focus entry/return behavior.
Antislop: R-32, R-39 (HIGH). Suggested workflow: impeccable harden, audit.
The visible close button works; this is not a claim that pointer users are trapped.

### 8. P1: Auto-moving homepage carousels lack persistent pause

Evidence: AutoCarousel continually advances, pauses during hover/focus and for a short grace period, and respects reduced motion. It has previous/next controls but no persistent pause mechanism.
Locations: src/lib/components/AutoCarousel.svelte:129, :137, :153, :250.
Impact: users cannot stop moving text while continuing to use the rest of the page.
Fix: provide a persistent pause/resume control or remove automatic movement where it does not help reading.
Antislop: R-19, R-39. Suggested workflow: impeccable harden, animate.
W3C SC 2.2.2 explains why focus-only pause does not satisfy a usable pause mechanism. Reduced-motion support is positive but does not replace it.

### 9. P2: Parent hierarchy favors setup over next lesson and learning evidence

Evidence: in the synthetic single-child 390px board, identity, access setup and counters fill the initial screen. Next lesson begins around y=845px and progress around y=1578px. Desktop progress begins around y=1285px. Source ordering agrees.
Locations: src/routes/app/parent/+page.svelte:461, :515, :552, :630.
Impact: the parent must scan administration before answering the product's progress question.
Fix: foreground next lesson, current learning focus and recent evidence; move repeat access setup and detailed finances into secondary areas.
Antislop: R-05, R-20, C-3 (LOW tier). Suggested workflow: impeccable shape, distill.
Synthetic geometry is not a claim about every production family or the multi-child overview.

### 10. P2: Student homework repeats progress before the assignments

Evidence: the populated synthetic homework view shows two consecutive progress cards with effectively the same submission summary when overall progress is homework-based. Source always renders both blocks.
Location: src/routes/app/student/+page.svelte:474, :483.
Impact: redundant scanning pushes the actual work down and can blur submission versus learning mastery.
Fix: show one compact submission summary; show distinct skill progress separately only when it is meaningfully different and clearly labelled.
Antislop: R-14, R-20, C-3 (purpose/composition). Suggested workflow: impeccable distill.
The two summaries may represent different concepts in a skill-based plan; do not indiscriminately remove that distinction.

### 11. P2: Related tutor pages lose journey context

Evidence: plan/report/editor return paths often lead to the global dashboard rather than the originating learner or filtered library. The standalone game catalog lacks a contextual back path unless a lesson was supplied.
Locations: src/routes/app/lessons/[slug]/edit/+page.svelte:304; src/routes/app/plan/[code]/+page.svelte:364; src/routes/app/report/[booking]/+page.svelte:166; src/routes/app/games/+page.svelte:43.
Impact: completing one small action entails searching for the previous context again.
Fix: preserve a safe origin and the relevant learner, lesson or library state, while retaining a separate global-dashboard route.
Antislop: R-20/C-2 continuity. Suggested workflow: impeccable shape.
These destinations exist; this is not a dead-link finding.

### 12. P2: Library polling failures silently preserve stale status

Source evidence: refresh ignores unsuccessful responses and catches connection failures without visible stale/error/retry state.
Location: src/routes/app/library/+page.svelte:37.
Impact: working/preparation status can look current even when it is not being refreshed.
Fix: keep last-known data explicitly labelled, announce refresh failure and offer retry.
Antislop: R-27 (HIGH rule tier; P2 UX impact in the bounded evidence). Suggested workflow: impeccable harden.
Production network failure was not injected.

### 13. P2: Homepage portal illustration lacks an explicit demo label

Evidence: the portal illustration contains a fictional learner and progress figures driven by hard-coded count-up targets. It is labelled as a parent portal but not explicitly as illustrative.
Locations: src/routes/+page.svelte:186, :287.
Impact: illustrative values and an older dashboard composition can be mistaken for actual product evidence.
Fix: label it as a demonstration or use an approved, privacy-safe current product example. Do not invent replacement numbers or people.
Antislop: R-17/R-38 honesty, R-31 purpose. Suggested workflow: impeccable clarify.
This is not an accusation that public testimonials are invented. PRODUCT identifies named tutors and real testimonials as intentional positioning.

### 14. P2: Booking copy conflicts with implemented confirmation expectations

Evidence: the details sheet says the tutor will return for final approval; implemented outcomes distinguish confirmed bookings, email approval and degraded/unconfirmed cases.
Location: src/routes/booking/+page.svelte:1060.
Impact: families may wait for a human confirmation instead of understanding the actual next step.
Fix: use state-appropriate wording describing who must act and how confirmation arrives.
Antislop: C-2/C-3, R-27 state clarity. Suggested workflow: impeccable clarify.
No booking or email confirmation was submitted.

### 15. P2: Shared error recovery describes unrelated routes as games

Live evidence: the original incomplete student URL reached the shared game-not-found page. Source uses game-specific 403/404 copy globally and sends recovery to the public homepage.
Location: src/routes/+error.svelte:44.
Impact: a tutor gets the wrong explanation and loses the route back to the roster.
Fix: contextual or neutral error copy and safe role/task-specific recovery.
Antislop: R-27, C-4. Suggested workflow: impeccable clarify, harden.
The original URL itself had a missing character; that typo is not an application defect.

## Cognitive load and personas

Tutor journeys fail single focus, immediate priority, progressive disclosure and context continuity. Five peer tabs overflow on phones; six equal dashboard metrics precede the roster; 138 editing controls are expanded in one sampled document. The library's 212 rows are not one simultaneous choice, but they conceal the next-task queue. Six on-demand learning-status choices are legitimate domain complexity, not inherently a defect.

- Frequent tutor: excessive traversal to locate preparation needs/save actions and repeated searching after navigation.
- Parent between errands: administration precedes evidence of learning; the synthetic single-child board requires scrolling before next lesson/progress.
- Keyboard-dependent user: hidden drawer focus, incomplete tutor-tab behavior and unnamed custom homework controls.
- Student navigation is comparatively manageable: four persistent destinations, with meaningful empty states and working task completion/undo in the synthetic check.

The emotional journey begins with identity and reassurance, then loses momentum when the next meaningful action is not evident. Learning maps and clear homework states restore context locally, but they do not yet form a coherent preparation/readiness story.

## What is working

- Student bottom navigation fits 320px/390px, has four clear destinations and updates current state.
- Synthetic homework completion and undo both updated the server-confirmed summary; targets were 44px high.
- Booking offers labelled 16px inputs, a meaningful dialog, Escape dismissal and preserved slot context.
- Scoped access kept a student out of parent finances rather than silently elevating permissions.
- Library has a labelled focusable scroll region, meaningful empty-filter recovery and 16px/44px filters.
- Editor draft/publish/version concepts and report suggestion approval are deliberately explained.
- Sampled legal pages have readable Hebrew prose and no page-wide phone overflow.
- One published lesson sample fit 390px, exposed 44px navigation and advanced correctly.
- Metrics labels unknown values honestly rather than inventing zeros.
- Existing semantic color-token tests pass in their actual bounded scope.

## Smaller observations, not a new polish backlog

Some tutor routes lack main landmarks; student home/games/question lack a consistent h1. Smaller input typography also appears in plan/report source. Delete-student is visually prominent beside everyday identity; homework deletion and charge cancellation have no source-defined undo/confirmation. These merit attention alongside the relevant numbered workflow, without inventing a separate aesthetic task list.

No automatic dark-mode expansion, cosmetic font replacement, emoji purge, metric fabrication or new navigation framework is recommended.
